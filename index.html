Our Story

Kritunga began with a single wood-fired vessel and a family recipe for Andhra-style biryani, carried from Guntur's spice lanes to Dilsukhnagar.

Every dish here is built the long way — ground masalas, slow-rendered ghee, rice aged a full season — so the heat of Andhra cooking arrives balanced, never blunt.

Rachit and the kitchen team treat each table as a guest in their own home, not a seat to turn over.
